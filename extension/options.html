🚨 Important Data Notice

If you uninstall this extension or reinstall Chrome, your local notes will be permanently lost! We highly recommend connecting Google Drive below, or occasionally exporting your canvas to a file to keep it safe.

Auto Backup (Local)

Local File Backup

Export saves your full canvas as a .json file you can store anywhere. Import restores a canvas from a previously exported file. A local backup is saved automatically before any import.

Google Drive Sync

Status: Not connected
Security & Privacy: Syncs your canvas data to an isolated folder in your personal drive. The extension has no access to any of your other Google Drive files. If disconnected, all canvas data remains stored locally.

Node Tabs

Canvas

Blurs all notes and images on the canvas until you click the Eye icon to reveal them. Perfect for screen sharing or working in public spaces.
Tip: Hide the "Customize Chrome" footer on the New Tab page
Right-click anywhere on the dark footer bar at the bottom of the new tab and select "Hide footer on New Tab page". Chrome will remember this preference!
